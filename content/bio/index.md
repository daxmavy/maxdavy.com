*Last updated 2026-07-20*

I studied a **Bachelors of Science (Advanced Mathematics)**, double-majoring in Mathematics and Computer Science at the University of Sydney, including an **Honours year in Applied Mathematics** (translation for non-Australians: this is an additional research year tacked onto the end of an undergraduate degree). My Honours thesis was on Discrete Morse Theory.[^morse]

I then worked for ~5 years, first at Atlassian (in their Ecosystem team), and then at Canva (in their Trust & Safety team). I had a great time and learned a lot about how to solve real-world problems with data at scale, and how to get stuff done.

However, the academic itch never left, so I studied a **MSc in Social Data Science** at the University of Oxford. I was lucky enough to get into the **DPhil in Social Data Science**, supported by the [Shirley Scholarship](https://www.oii.ox.ac.uk/about/giving/the-shirley-scholars-fund/) (from the OII) and the [BOLD Lab](https://bold-lab.ai/). I will start in Oct 2026, and am part of Exeter College.

Effective altruism has influenced me for a long time, primarily because it takes seriously the imperative for those in privileged positions to do good with their life. I think AI is likely to be transformative, and it is important for it to go well. However, I am somewhat skeptical of the [maximalist tendencies and logics](/maximalism/) that EA can encourage.
